Victoria Adler

actor




Victoria Adler, a Swedish actor from the small town of Enköping. From her formative years, she immersed herself fully in narratives, whether films or books. She often daydreamed about the lives of the various characters. Little did she know this was the spark to her interest in acting. Over time, films like "The Lord of the Rings," "Troy," "The Last Samurai," and "Gladiator" not only captured her imagination but also ignited her fascination with swordsmanship. Additionally, BBC's rendition of "Robin Hood" came along and prompted her exploration of archery and historical reenactment.

At 15, Victoria discovered that acting was the gateway to participating in these captivating narratives. Her role as Juliet in Shakespeare's "Romeo and Juliet" marked her stage debut and propelled her forward without a moment's hesitation. Subsequently, this led her to audition to the acting program at Stockholm Art School (2010-2013), where she got accepted. Alongside her studies, Victoria entered the world of film. From roles in short films to numerous appearances as an extra. This opened up her eyes to the profession and she never looked back. She was determined to become a part of it. The next move was relocating to Boston, MA after graduating. Where she worked as an Au Pair while pursuing acting studies at JRP New England.

After a few years of gaining life experience, she started to audition for a number of drama schools. She secured a spot at The School of the Science of Acting (Kogan Academy of Dramatic Arts) 2017. This brought her to London and the city that has been her main base for the last 7 years. Graduating into a very different industry in 2020, Victoria has since then embodied several roles on stage and in front of the camera.

At present, Victoria is coaching archery at Archery Fit as well as improving her skills and knowledge. Coming from a physical background, encompassing football, horse riding and dance, she thrives in physically demanding roles.
She is also working as an acting coach alongside auditioning and working on her craft.

Representation and professional collaboration offers are welcome.
